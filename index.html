Part 3: Systemic fix (the tool)

BlueDot Round Operations (three modes in one file)

Single-file HTML tool with a mode switcher. Dashboard is the Monday-morning landing view, showing every active round at a glance with open action-required flags, status by cohort, upcoming launches, and a cohort heatmap. Launching a round is the pre-launch checklist (seven sections per cohort, edge cases, issue log, failure response panel). Mid-round check is a weekly six-section health check (pulse, attendance, facilitator quality, Slack, prior-week issues, comms) with per-cohort trend charts, an escalation guide, and round close-out with a three-question retrospective. State persists in localStorage. Markdown export per mode.
